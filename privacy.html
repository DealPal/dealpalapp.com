1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Deal Pal Inc. ("Deal Pal," "we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your information when you use the Deal Pal™ mobile application and related services ("App").

1.2 By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy and consent to the practices described herein, subject to applicable law.

1.3 This Policy complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other applicable privacy laws.

1.4 If you do not agree with this Policy, please discontinue use of the App.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

(a) Account Information

• Name (optional)

• Username

• Email address

• Password (hashed and encrypted)

• Optional profile photo

• Account creation date

(b) Location Data

• Precise GPS location (only with explicit device permission)

• Approximate location

Location data is used solely to display nearby deals. The App does not access precise location without your explicit opt-in through device permissions.

(c) User-Generated Content

• Deal posts (title, price, store, quantity, general location info)

• Photos uploaded to the App

• Comments, replies, likes, and interaction metrics

(d) Device and Technical Information

• Device model and operating system version

• IP address

• App version

• Mobile network information

• Temporary, app-scoped identifiers (e.g., session IDs used for security and analytics)

We do not use IDFA or engage in cross-app tracking.

(e) Usage and Analytics Data

• Features and pages used

• Session duration

• Timestamps of activity

• Crash logs and performance metrics

• Search queries

(f) Local Storage Technologies

• App storage for settings and preferences

• Functionality-related analytics storage

Some features may not function properly if storage is disabled.

5. DATA RETENTION

5.1 User-Generated Content Visibility vs. Storage

Deal posts, comments, and images expire and are removed from public display after approximately 24 hours.

Copies of user-generated content may be retained on secure servers for a limited and reasonable period for moderation, abuse prevention, legal compliance, and system integrity. Such content is not publicly visible and is deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable law.

5.2 Account Information

• Retained while the account is active

• Deleted within 30 days of account deletion unless legally required

5.3 Security Logs

Minimal security-related logs may be retained for fraud prevention and compliance.
